# Ash & Oath v0.37 — two Trial classes

**Main menu → Trials → tap the class card.** Each of the three named heroes may choose either class, including repeated classes. Previewing another class does not change equipment; **Use** applies its kit to the selected hero only. New parties default to Skirmisher / Vanguard / Skirmisher.

| Class | Starting Weapon | Starting Body | Starting Memory | Maximum HP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skirmisher | Spear → rolled Jab | Fleet Body | Memory of Momentum | 4 (5 − 1) |
| Vanguard | Hammer → rolled Crack | Stone Body | Memory of Guard | 6 (7 − 1) |

Both include **Beginning Soul**, converting one rolled Blank to Move 1 per own turn. Starting equipment costs no Essence. A class supplies its Body for that hero without unlocking the crafting recipe globally; crafting that Body makes it available to the other class. Class names add no hidden stat bonus beyond equipped gear.

- **Skirmisher:** Fleet Body makes Step move 2 and Stride move 4. Move first, then a rolled Jab reaches an enemy within 2; Memory of Momentum adds 1 to the first eligible own-turn Attack after moving two spaces. Fragile at 4 maximum HP. Retained Strikes and Strike Numeric give ordinary attacks; Momentum Strike is on the third die. Face rewards are Momentum Strike, Retreat and Leap of Echoes.
- **Vanguard:** use Guard, Iron Shelter or a held Reaction to block an enemy Attack fully and prime Memory of Guard’s +1 next-Attack damage. During your turn, use another rolled Attack before Crack removes up to 2 adjacent enemy Block. Crack deals no HP damage. Stone Body shortens Stride to 2. Starter dice retain normal Strikes, Strike Numeric and Engrave to set up Crack. Face rewards are Iron Shelter, Evasive Counter and Wither Ward.

The class page shows every side of all three starter dice, with category colors, tappable effect descriptions and an example turn. Jab / Crack replace exactly one normal Strike side, must be rolled and consume that die, once per own turn; invalid attempts spend nothing. Other Strikes stay available. Two Blanks per die and all normal category/copy constraints remain.

You may adjust owned gear before starting; the class card marks **adjusted gear** and calculates actual HP. Memory changes do not rewrite class dice. Choosing a class again restores its starting gear, including Beginning Soul. Existing rewards can refit the run; classes cannot change mid-run. Class identity is visible in combat and equipment help. Movement Details show the equipped Body’s actual distance.

Class choices and equipment persist on this device. Old safe checkpoints keep their actual equipment, dice, HP, route and collections; their next new Trial offers the classes. Ordinary Camp, normal game setup, inventories, the 73 normal face IDs and 48 free bonus designs stay unchanged. Current rules/HTML/PDF and in-game notes include class definitions and exact dice. Windows downloads were not rebuilt. Balance remains experimental.
