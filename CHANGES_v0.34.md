# Ash & Oath v0.34 — Trials

Main menu → **Trials** opens a separate experimental expedition. Phone users also have a fixed Trials shortcut on the main menu. The existing combat system, normal builds, Camp resources, 73-face catalog and 48 free bonus designs remain available.

Choose **Venom pact**, **Iron vow**, or **Wayfarer** for each hero, plus one of the four final bosses. Venom rewards a first damaging Attack with Poison but reduces healing. Iron vow rewards a fully blocked hit with a counterattack bonus, at 5 maximum HP. Wayfarer rewards moving two spaces before attacking, also at 5 maximum HP. Wayfarer starts with Shove for terrain tactics. All 27 path combinations obey normal dice construction limits.

Five branching stops precede the boss. Choose regular fights or elites, Camp or combat, and a safe or risky cache. HP carries forward. Camp restores 2 HP to every hero, including fallen heroes, or grants 2 Trial Essence. Safe route/reward checkpoints resume locally; leaving or reloading during a fight ends that Trial with no victory reward.

Three original enemy groups test different decisions:

- **Ward & Whisper:** Shieldbearer protects nearby allies; Mender repairs injured allies. Shield Line adds 2 Block, capped at 3; Restitch heals up to 2 once per enemy turn. Separate them or focus the Mender. Blue cover reduces ranged hits by 1.
- **The Long Charge:** Ram marks a fixed lane that hits for 3 at the next enemy turn start. Sidestep the gold warning or defend it. A Sentry provides ranged pressure. Brown barricades block movement and sight; spend an adjacent Attack to break one.
- **Essence Thieves:** Cutpurses steal carried Trial Essence and flee toward the edge. Kill one to recover its loot; escaped loot is lost. Landing on red spikes deals 1 unavoidable damage, including Shove targets.

Each enemy rolls public dice and exposes live intent details. Elites add a third role and +1 HP per enemy. Trial Essence remains visible during combat, and the field guide explains perks, terrain, public dice, stolen loot and ready bonuses. Combat and Trial menu exits stay fixed on phones.

Start with 3 Trial Essence. Victory grants 2 for a regular group or 4 for an elite. Then choose exactly one reward: pay 2 to install a path ability on a legal chosen side; pay 2 to temper an Attack; or save the offer as +2 Essence. Venom edge trades 1 direct damage for Poison on HP-damaging hits. Heavy edge adds 1 damage but costs 1 HP after the Attack. Edges belong to physical sides, respect Numeric zero/attack caps, and disappear if that side is replaced. Illegal replacements cost nothing. A boss victory records 6 Trial Essence and unlocks a Soul for future Trials.

Each boss unlocks an original **Trial Soul** with a tradeoff:

| Boss | Soul | Effect and cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow | Cinder | First damaging Attack each own turn adds Poison 1; start each encounter with Poison 1. |
| Nu’gh | Brood | First Poison application each own turn spreads Poison 1 to another adjacent enemy; maximum HP -1. |
| Veyl | Echo | First Skill each own turn grants +1 next-Attack damage; start with no Action Reroll. |
| Bu’gh | Warden | Preserve up to 2 Block at own-turn start; Movement distances -1, minimum 1. |

Trial Souls are selected before a run. Every hero still has one rolled Blank as Move 1 per own turn. Existing Debug mode supports stationary holds on locked Trial Soul cards in the field guide. Trial unlocks never write normal progression.

These are original experimental designs, separate from unfinished PDF services/Hunts. Difficulty needs human playtesting, especially elite routes and low-HP parties. Actual Chromium and emulated-touch checks accompany native regressions and exploratory simulations; physical phone hardware was not tested. Existing pixel art is reused. Windows downloads were not rebuilt.
