# Ash & Oath v0.29 — Creatures of the Wilds

Three original ordinary enemies, three enemy-specific Essence types, three bound Souls and six new sprites expand the existing progression. These are new playtest designs requested for the game, not claims about the supplied PDF or its unfinished service/Hunt/boss candidates.

| Enemy | Tactics | Direct encounter | Harvest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mire Leech | 4 HP. Scuttle 2; Guard 1; Venom Bite deals 1 at reach 1 and adds Poison 1 only after actual HP loss. Normal Reactions can prevent it. | The Sunken Bank: two Leeches. | One Mire Leech Essence per defeated individual. |
| Glasswing Moth | 3 HP. Scuttle 2; Guard 1; Needle deals 1 at range 3 with line of sight, including after Reactions. | Glasswing Swarm: two Moths. | One Glasswing Moth Essence per defeated individual. |
| Iron Husk | 5 HP. Scuttle 1; Bash 2 at reach 1; Iron Guard grants 2 Block. No passive armor. | Rusted Procession: one Husk and one existing Movement Creature. | One Iron Husk Essence per defeated individual; its escort gives Movement Essence. |

All have their own three public six-sided dice and one-cell footprints. Dice and current results remain visible on Enemy cards. Victory requires defeating every enemy. Victory banks individual tokens once; defeat, interruption or leaving discards only current tokens, preserving the prior bank. These direct groups award Essence only and do not resolve or replace the required pre-boss event. Earlier groups, the six-event table and all four bosses remain.

| Soul | Craft/bind cost | Blank ability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soul of the Mire | 3 Mire Leech Essence per recipient. | Spend one rolled Blank to heal yourself 1 HP, once per own turn. Requires missing HP; cannot revive a Downed owner. No movement. Actual self-healing can receive Memory of Loss’s once-per-combat bonus. |
| Glasswing Soul | 3 Glasswing Moth Essence per recipient. | Spend one rolled Blank to blink up to 2 Manhattan spaces, once per own turn. Occupied intermediate cells do not block it; landing must be legal and empty. Rooted prevents it. No movement in addition to the blink. |
| Ironbound Soul | 3 Iron Husk Essence per recipient. | Spend one rolled Blank for 2 Block, once per own turn. Normal Block modifiers/expiry apply. No movement. |

In Essence Forge, use More to view the new sources, select the recipient and bind the Soul. Its exact name/effect reveals on the first craft. A hero can own each Soul once; other recipients each pay the same full cost. Souls never trade. Binding does not auto-equip: use Camp → Equipment → Soul. Equipment’s More Souls gallery shows the new sprites. Existing body/category/Blank/ownership limits and one equipped Soul per hero remain. No new free bonus faces or changed face IDs were added.

The three new abilities share the normal per-hero Blank-conversion allowance. Successful use spends that Blank and the once-per-own-turn allowance; invalid destinations, full-HP Mire attempts and Rooted blink attempts spend nothing. Further Blanks can still be declined or resolved without a Soul benefit. Normal and full recovery turns refresh the allowance; rerolls, shared rolls, options toggles and borrowed single-action interrupts do not. The new Souls cannot activate through Shared Burden. The separate sandbox override can still test ordinary Blank movement and restores equipped Soul rules when turned off.

Camp keeps spacious cards by paging both the creature and Essence source lists. Equipment retains existing sprites and adds a second gallery for the new Souls. Original transparent enemy/Soul artwork is stored in one atlas and used through six AtlasTexture resources. No external artwork was copied.

HP, dice weights, encounter composition and Soul costs/effects are provisional. Tests verify implementation, not human balance. Undefined Forge services, campaign Hunt scheduling and bespoke boss opportunities remain future content. Windows downloads were not rebuilt.
