# Ash & Oath v0.38 — Soulcaster and clearer Trials

12 November 2026. Gameplay additions are confined to Trials.

- **Soulcaster is a free starter Soul** for either class. Select a hero → Soul → Soulcaster before Begin Trial. Starter dice adapt to four Blanks each; the Weapon still projects its Strike into Jab / Crack. Six base HP applies before Memory penalties; No Memory keeps six with no automatic bonus.
- **Gather and Manifest:** Use a ready Blank to gather a Soul Charge (cap 3), or create a spell for charges and 1 HP. Four spell instances per caster. The exact physical Blank changes; the current result stays spent. A spell cannot activate during its creation turn, even after a reroll, and must be rolled on a later turn. Charges begin at zero each encounter; spells reset to the run build after it.
- **Six spells from the supplied PDF:** Wraith Exchange, Soul Harvester, Mirror of the Damned, Grave Stitch, Hoarded Omen and Fatebreaker. The dedicated picker and spellbook show creation costs, activation costs, range, legal targets and restrictions.
- **Hoarded Omen uses the user's selected interpretation:** the original die stays aside, skips the next shared roll and releases its saved result on the target's own turn in the following round. It does not add a fourth die.
- **Fatebreaker** spends its when-rolled result, grants one Action Reroll up to the existing cap and rerolls an unresolved actual enemy die. Trial creatures are eligible before the boss. Each enemy die can be rerolled once per shared round; when none is eligible, keep only the granted Action Reroll. Initial Bind effects wait for the final results.
- **Mirror** grants its target 2 Block before an Attack and reflects only after the whole Attack causes no HP loss, including collision riders. It can protect the living Spirit or respond to a legal friendly Attack. Harvester's kill refund excludes ritual/core objects. Grave Stitch can revive a hero, respects existing healing modifiers and returns its own side to Blank.
- **Short help topics:** how to play, selected hero's four equipment slots and actual dice, rewards/resources, arena, boss Souls and Soulcaster. Body / Memory / Soul / Weapon have plain-language roles. Charge and spell counts appear in combat.
- **Resources have distinct explanations:** Run Essence buys this run's upgrades; matching Forge materials persist for future crafting; Soul Charges pay for combat spells; Action Rerolls are another bank.
- **Reward purchases have a review step:** select a side, read its replacement and price, then Buy. Unavailable slots explain Blank, category, weapon or copy restrictions without spending currency. Read-only previews never change the shared copy pool.
- **Rerolls refresh ability hints** and keep the existing nonblocking dice presentation.

Normal encounters, Camp inventories, 73 normal faces, 48 free designs, four bosses and two class defaults remain. Existing safe checkpoints retain their actual builds. Windows artifacts were not rebuilt. Trial balance still needs player feedback; browser checks do not substitute for physical phone testing.
