# Ash & Oath v0.42 — Guided preparation and faster dice

14 November 2026

- Added Settings → Dice animation & pause. OFF skips the dice animation and its result hold in both modes. Rolled faces, last result and full history remain; calculations and RNG run once. The preference persists alongside Blank → Move 1 without overwriting it.
- Added a short collapse, ash burst and low sound when a unit falls. Downed and slain heroes have distinct cues. Outcomes wait for the fall; damage, protection, revival, rewards and occupied-cell rules are unchanged.
- Updated the battle HUD with sharper frames, health bars and colored face marks. Intent names / targets and boss status have separate bounded slots. Fixed status / log overlap, long intent overflow, long enemy-action headings and cramped preparation face cards.
- Replaced the crowded Trial lobby with six focused steps per hero: Class, Soul, Body, Weapon, Memory, Dice. Back and reload preserve choices. Each hero reviews all 18 real starting sides, including Weapon replacements. After three reviews, inspect the party, choose the boss and Begin. The first Memory remains empty.
- Retained four-slot equipment rules, class/Soul ownership, normal / Trial isolation, existing creatures and dice mechanics. Regenerated current rules as HTML, Markdown and PDF.

Browser release; existing Windows downloads remain unchanged. Saves and preferences stay local to this browser/device.
