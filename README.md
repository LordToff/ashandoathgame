# Ash & Oath v0.29

[Play in your browser](https://lordtoff.github.io/ashandoathgame/?v=0.29)

Three new enemies—Mire Leech, Glasswing Moth and Iron Husk—have distinct public dice, original sprites, matching Essence and three craftable bound Souls. Spend a rolled Blank once per own turn to heal, blink or gain Block. Camp pages keep the larger encounter/source/Soul lists readable. Existing bosses, events, Dagger, Beginning Soul, earlier recipes and banks remain. New values and prices are provisional. Windows is unchanged.
