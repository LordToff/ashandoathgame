# v0.31 — play in a phone browser

Choose **Mobile mode · play on a phone** on the main menu. Small touch screens use the phone layout automatically unless this browser has a saved preference. Choose Desktop mode to switch back.

- Menus, Camp, equipment, Essence crafting and dice editing use large controls and vertical scrolling.
- In battle, tap a die and then its highlighted destination or target. Use Resolve for immediate effects. Ability details and party status buttons provide information without hovering.
- Swipe the battle controls to reach more actions. Portrait puts them below the board; landscape puts them alongside it. Rotation preserves the fight.
- Reactions, copying, rewards, enemy cards, supplies and the full battle log use scrollable panels. Summary and Extensive log views remain available.
- Recorded dice animations fit the phone viewport. Layout preference saves separately from earned rewards.

This is the same browser game and rule system. Equipment, face limits, reward ownership and combat timing are unchanged. There is no app to install. Saves remain local to this browser; switching phones does not transfer them automatically. Advanced balance/Ability Lab tools remain available in the desktop layout. Windows downloads are unchanged.

Validated in Chromium with emulated touch, at 390×844, 360×640 and 844×390, plus desktop regression checks. Physical iOS/Android devices have not been tested here.
