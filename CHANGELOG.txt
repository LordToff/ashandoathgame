# Ash & Oath v0.27 — Essence and the Camp Forge

The supplied Essence Harvesting & Prototype Enemies v1 is now implemented as a playable progression prototype. [Full implementation and clarifications](Essence-Implementation-v0.27.md) · [original design PDF](design/Essence-Harvesting-and-Prototype-Enemies-v1.pdf).

- Every eligible individual enemy death creates one enemy-specific Essence token on its defeated card. Victory banks the tokens; defeat discards only the current encounter’s tokens. Banked Essence is safe until voluntarily spent. Claims cannot duplicate on redraw/reload.
- Conditions check state at death, including before death cleanup. Normal eligibility is public; boss requirements and rewards reveal after first defeat. The three new creatures use regular harvesting. Existing boss mappings are provisionally regular until their specific requirements/recipes are defined.
- Camp Forge has the exact nine reward faces and Memory of Loss from the PDF at their listed costs. Exact effects/names reveal on first craft, which grants copy one. Additional face copies cost the same, with no owned maximum or mutual exclusivity. Memory is a one-time permanent unlock. There is no dismantling/refund.
- Refit crafted faces among heroes while retaining two Blanks per die, body/category limits, the two selected bonus designs, and actual shared copy counts. Unequipped faces remain owned. Saved build presets check owned copies and party-wide allocation.
- Souls bind to recipients, can be swapped only among that hero’s owned Souls, and never trade. Tutorial Hollow awards its Soul to all three heroes. The Fragment event now binds its unlock to the chosen hero; older unlocks migrate without losing entitlements. The manual Blank-movement sandbox override remains clearly separate.
- Event 4 introduces two Bone Creatures; event 5 introduces a Support Creature and two Movement Creatures. Stats, per-creature public dice and group compositions are explicitly provisional. Enemy cards show their current results and harvesting state. Three original pixel placeholders were added.
- Bone results retain spent rolls in their count. Shared Remedy requires actual self-healing before its ally heal. Sorrow’s Offering pays HP for an ally buff. Shared Burden uses a documented immediate single-action ally interrupt and returns to the owner without extra activation/turn-start effects. Memory of Loss applies its Healing exceptions and once-per-combat self-heal bonus. Retreat checks every enemy; Momentum checks movement; Evasive Counter steps only after a fully resolved Attack removes no HP.

The four bosses, initial 48-design bonus pool, original 60 face IDs, Blank progression, Summon/Command Nu’gh, stacking Poison, reroll timing choices, soundtrack and earlier systems remain. New earned face IDs are 60–68. Existing Gold, Salves, Seals and Fragments remain event rewards, but cannot substitute for Essence in crafting. Windows downloads are unchanged.

Cleansing, Reforging, Restoration, Transmutation, Empowerment, Hunt scheduling and unique-boss Essence opportunities remain future content because their effects, unlocks or scheduling were not finalized in the PDF. No separate spendable Bone currency or invented universal pricing schedule was added. Enemy AI, Shared Burden timing and duplicate/Bone scaling are playtest implementations, not proof of balance.

Current HTML, Markdown, PDF, in-game patch notes, source design reference, tests and reproducible local browser fixtures are included. Validation details are recorded separately.

# Ash & Oath v0.26 — combat clarity

- **Poison stacks:** 1 + 1 = 2. At activation it deals damage equal to current Poison, bypassing Block, then decreases by one. For example, Poison 2 deals 2 damage, then the next activation Poison 1 deals 1. Downing/death still clears it. A Continue window shows actual HP damage, old/new HP and remaining Poison, including bosses and summons.
- **Borrow Shape uses face-up dice:** choose any hero's visible result, including a spent die or held Reaction. Resolve a copy on the Borrow Shape die; leave the source die unchanged. The source does not need to be unresolved. Borrow Shape itself and Red Tile are excluded to prevent copy loops and copying a locked penalty.
- **Action Rerolls default to any time during the active hero's turn.** Options provides an Opening only toggle for comparison. Charges, pending decisions, forced resolutions and spent/Channel restrictions remain. Held Reactions may be rerolled, removing the old reaction latch before rolling the new result.
- **Focus and Action Rerolls show their outcome.** Focus still costs its own die, not a charge. Select Focus then another eligible die; the result window reports old/new sides and abilities. A same-face result is explicitly explained. The Action Reroll button can also enter target-selection mode if no die is selected.
- **Reaction accounting:** the default is still 2 Block, granted before incoming normal damage consumes it. Tests cover hits of 1, 2 and 3 damage. A 2-damage hit leaves zero Block but removes no HP. The prompt shows current versus pre-hit Block; the log records the grant separately from consumption and HP damage. Custom Balance Workshop values still apply; the change does not overwrite profiles.
- **Summary and Extensive full log tabs:** both side and expanded views expose these tabs. Extensive selects the All filter and includes every recorded event chronologically. The old 2,000-entry truncation is removed. Info, roll results, reaction decisions and Poison ticks remain available; Summary omits explanatory info and turn guides.
- **Main menu restart:** Options returns to the first hero's face selection, abandoning combat and clearing combat overlays. Earned inventory and audio/settings remain; this does not erase the local ledger.
- **Events precede bosses:** Begin expedition leads to an event, then Enter selected boss. Starting another boss attempt or rematching requires another event. Safe event options remain legal; event costs and rewards are unchanged.

Browser-only release; the older Windows download is unchanged. The prior game rules deliberately used refresh-only Poison, opening-only rerolls and the rotate-a-die Borrow Shape variant; those behaviors are now changed according to this feedback, not silently retained as compatibility requirements.

The focused regression fixture is `prototype/tests/usability_v26_test.gd`. Historical Poison and opening-window assertions were updated to exercise the new stacking rule and the explicit Opening only mode rather than removing coverage. Current consolidated HTML, Markdown and PDF are generated from the updated current-rule source.

# Ash & Oath v0.25 — The Ash Road

The first optional event loop sits beside the four existing standalone boss encounters. From boss review, open **Expedition events**. All six numbered slots are visible. Roll one d6, read the selected event’s stakes, then choose. The result cannot be rolled again until it is resolved; **New sortie** deliberately starts another event.

| d6 | Event | Choices and exact rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Abandoned Supplies | Take 1 Salve **or** 1 Reroll Seal. No cost or risk. |
| 2 | Ash Shrine | Leave safely, or pay 2 Gold now. Roll 3–6: gain 1 Soul Fragment; 1–2: offering lost, no reward. |
| 3 | Stranded Traveller | Take directions for 1 Gold, or spend 1 Salve to earn 3 Gold + 1 Reroll Seal. |
| 4 | Scavenger Ambush | Withdraw safely, or defeat two 3-HP Ash Scavengers for 3 Gold + 1 Salve. |
| 5 | Infested Ruins | Leave safely, or defeat two 4-HP Cinder Mites for 5 Gold + 1 Soul Fragment + 1 Reroll Seal. |
| 6 | Buried Reliquary | Take 1 Gold, or replace it with a search. Roll 4–6: 3 Gold + 1 Soul Fragment; 1–3: lose 1 held Salve and gain nothing. With no Salve, failure grants nothing. |

## Rewards have uses

- Shared expedition **Gold** funds shrine offerings. There is no shop. Gambler’s Coin Gold remains encounter-local.
- A **Salve** heals the active standing hero by up to 2 HP, capped at maximum HP, at most once per own turn. It cannot revive.
- A **Reroll Seal** restores 1 stored Action Reroll if below the existing bank cap. The reroll itself still follows the normal rules.
- Supplies consume no Action Die. Finish pending decisions first. Open **Menu → Expedition supplies** during combat. Supplies are unavailable in Lab and training.
- Spend **3 Soul Fragments** once to unlock the existing optional reward Soul. Its default is one rolled Blank per hero turn as Move 2. There is no free movement and installed Blanks remain Blanks. Select Blank movement on boss review; existing manual rules options and numeric overrides remain.

## Ordinary monsters, without a boss

Both event fights have two separate one-cell ordinary monsters. They share three public Action Dice, resolving each result for both living monsters, with held Reactions interrupting before damage:

- Die I: Scuttle / Scuttle / Bite / Bite / Guard / Bite.
- Die II: Scuttle / Bite / Guard / Bite / Scuttle / Bite.
- Die III: Bite / Guard / Scuttle / Bite / Guard / Scuttle.

Scuttle moves up to 2 orthogonal cells toward enemies without attacking. Bite deals 1 normal damage to the closest adjacent hero or friendly summon, otherwise holds. Guard grants 1 Block until next enemy turn. No Poison on bites, boss phase, boss summons, fire or boss Soul. Hero summons and inflicted Poison still work. **Both monsters must die** to earn the listed reward; party defeat or abandoning grants none.

## Claims, saving and scope

The local ledger records event selection, exact grants, deductions and completion state. A repeated click, redraw or reload cannot claim the same event reward again. Interrupted monster encounters give no completion reward; spent supplies stay spent. Combat is not checkpointed. Boss and event fights start fresh: event injuries and temporary mutations do not carry into the next fight. Only earned inventory persists.

Storage belongs to the current browser/device, not a cloud account. Clearing browser data removes it. Explicit new sorties may earn more rewards; no daily restriction or full campaign economy is implied. Rewards and difficulty are provisional.

All four bosses, face ownership/caps, Blank progression, friendly Summon/Command Nu’gh, soundtrack and hover descriptions remain. The Windows download is unchanged. The consolidated current Markdown, HTML and PDF include the event table, odds, exact quantities, monster dice and persistence limits.

- Event rules: **355 checks passed**, covering costs, both risk outcomes, exact grants, duplicate claims, save/reload, interrupted fights, supply limits, two-monster victory, Reactions and tooltips.
- Event UI fixture: **24 checks passed**, including entry from boss review, six visible slots, locked roll, unaffordable choices, actual outcome grants and selected-boss preservation.
- **24 existing core regression scripts passed**: rules, catalog, presentation/pairs, lab, update, Soul, balance, boss v13, loadout, bug fixes v15, presets, encounters v18/v19, Cantor, brood, Numeric, summons, worm routing, status sweep, Warden, reliability, polish, playtest v12 and trial regressions.

# Ash & Oath v0.24 — Bu’gh, the Iron Warden

Bu’gh’s cocoon-and-mite encounter has been replaced. The fourth encounter now rewards guarding, reading a fixed area warning and moving to create an opening. The Hollow, Nu’gh, Veyl, friendly Summon/Command Nu’gh, the original soundtrack and the polished interface remain available. The older Windows build is unchanged.

## Guard or evade, then counterattack

Bu’gh has 12 HP and Bulwark, reducing normal incoming damage by 1. A positive-damage attack fully prevented by a hero’s Block or Damage Reduction breaks Bulwark and removes all boss Block. A threatened hero escaping a marked sweep also exposes him when it resolves. Counterattack during the opening; Bulwark returns at his next activation. Defy Death alone, friendly-worm defenses and zero-damage attacks do not grant openings. Unavoidable damage bypasses Bulwark.

Golden cells show the exact sweep area and damage. Warnings remain fixed if the boss moves or changes phase. Impact resolves before his next three dice, with Reactions; movement Reactions can escape the marked cells. A sweep that never threatened a hero grants no free opening. Every completed boss turn leaves a warning if one is not already armed. Warnings do not stack.

## Six abilities and phase II

- **Iron Advance:** Move 1 → 2.
- **Shield Bash:** range 1, damage 1 → 2.
- **Hammerfall:** area damage 2, reach 1 → 2.
- **Raise Guard:** Block 1 → 2. Cannot restore broken Bulwark early.
- **Crushing Sweep:** fixed warning reach 1 → 2, damage 1 → 2.
- **Driving Blow:** damage 1 → 2; HP-damaging hits push 1 → 2. Full guard prevents the push.

Phase II starts at half HP and cannot be reversed by healing. Active values are exposed in Balance Workshop. Old cocoon override keys remain compatible with saved profiles but are inactive and hidden from current boss controls. Original 32×32 shield-and-hammer sprites replace the former boss artwork, including an ember-cracked second phase.

## Validation and limits

The focused Warden mechanics suite passes 38 checks, including perfect versus partial guard, Damage Reduction, actual Reactions, movement Reactions, fixed warning timing, phase snapshots, multi-target damage, pushes, friendly worms, Poison and legacy balance overrides. The UI fixture exercises encounter selection, warning text, board movement input, explicit sweep resolution, opening indicators and phase-II warnings.

The capped mixed-build trials reached the bounded simulation limit; they are not counted as victories. These are limited heuristic bots, not human win rates or proof of balance. Results show a benefit from defense and movement, but difficulty remains provisional and demanding. The initial, more attack-heavy Warden was reduced before release. # v0.22.1 — Friendly Nu’gh movement fix

A friendly worm could activate after its summoner’s turn without moving. Its movement search considered only destinations within its two-step allowance and preferred staying still whenever every short detour was initially farther from an enemy. A line of heroes between the worm and boss could trap it this way indefinitely, despite an open route around them.

Friendly worms now plan the complete shortest legal route to an enemy-adjacent tile, then move only the first two orthogonal steps. They can take a detour that initially increases their distance. Command and automatic owner-end activations share this fix. They still stop adjacent and bite after movement; Rooted, occupancy, movement allowance and genuinely blocked routes remain enforced. A truly blocked worm explains the lack of a legal route in the chronicle.

The exact three-hero-line reproduction now moves from F5 to F7 after the summoner’s turn, then reaches D7 and bites on its next activation. Other heroes’ turns do not activate the worm. Existing enemy Nu’gh worms and Bu’gh mites keep their prior movement behavior; no balance values or declared rules changed.

Validation: 75 new movement checks, 5 rendered input/animation checks, the 298-check summon suite, Nu’gh’s 2,414-check brood regression and the 28-check encounter/summon UI suite pass. Browser verification uses the actual export and clicks End hero turn to confirm both the changed model tile and visible worm movement. The v0.22 trial report remains historical; its results are not new hotfix balance estimates. Bu’gh remains provisional.

The existing v0.22 rulebook still describes the intended Move 2 behavior. The older Windows download is unchanged.

# v0.22 — The Cocoon Regent and Summon Nu’gh

Reconstructed from the development handoff on 1 November 2026. The available prototype branch ended at published source `a8884c3d`; no uncommitted v0.22 files were available in the replacement workspace.

The Cocoon Regent joins The Hollow, Nu’gh and Veyl. Original integer-grid pixel art depicts its two phases, silk cocoons and mites. No Soul Link.

- **Lay the Cradle:** lay a cocoon on an empty cell within 2; enemy brood cap 3.
- **Shed the Shell:** gain 1 Block and lay a cocoon; phase II gains 2 Block.
- **Silken Mandate:** highest-HP standing hero in range 4 suffers Weakness; phase II also Roots until next boss turn.
- **Hatching Scream:** hatch the oldest cocoon; phase II hatches all.
- **Royal Feast:** consume an enemy mite to heal 1; phase II also gains 1 Block. Friendly worms are never food.
- **Carapace Crash:** move 1, reach 1, single-target damage 2. Phase II: move 2, reach 2, area damage 1.

Bu’gh starts at 12 HP with one cocoon. Cocoons have 1 HP and hatch after three brood phases. New phase-II cocoons have 2 HP; existing cocoons keep their HP. Mites have 1 HP, Move 2 (3 in phase II), reach 1 and Numeric 1,1,0,0,0,0. Phase-II newborn mites gain 1 Block. Successful HP-damaging mite bites inflict Weakness; phase II also Roots until the next boss turn. Mites activate after all three boss dice, with real Reaction windows between bites.

A hero destroying a cocoon gains 1 Block, removes up to 2 boss Block and inflicts boss Weakness until Bu’gh’s turn ends. Cocoon destruction causes no direct boss HP damage.

## New selectable Skill: Summon Nu’gh (ID 59)

Summon one small friendly Nu’gh onto an adjacent empty tile: 1 HP, Move 2, bite reach 1, Numeric 1,1,0,0,0,0. HP-damaging bites apply Poison 1. After its owner’s turn it moves toward enemies and attacks the closest enemy in reach. While it lives, all copies owned by that hero display **Command Nu’gh**, granting an immediate extra activation. This does not remove its normal owner-end activation. Death restores Summon. The maximum is one living worm per hero across all copies. Friendly sprites carry the owner’s colored ring and V/R/W marker.

The catalog now has 60 IDs and 48 selectable bonus designs. Two distinct bonus choices per hero, default two Blanks per die, ownership and category limits remain in place.

## Factions and Poison

Friendly and enemy summons are distinct for targeting, movement occupancy, brood caps, consumption, Cleave splash, area damage, poison, deaths and logs. Enemies can kill friendly worms. Nu’gh’s existing enemy brood, Soul Link phase break, Commands and automatic brood turn remain; friendly worms do not strengthen or feed them.

Poison can affect heroes, bosses and summons. HP damage applies it; blocked bites do not. Further applications refresh rather than stack. At the victim’s next activation, Poison causes unavoidable HP damage and clears. Downing clears Poison. Cocoons resolve it before aging or hatching in the brood phase.

New numeric settings are exposed in Balance Workshop. Numeric animations use recorded die results. Ability descriptions remain hover-only; hero movement keeps its existing tile paths and animation.

## Validation and limits

See [validation details](Validation-v0.22.md) and [raw trial scenarios](Trial-results-v0.22.json). This is a provisional balance release, **not a claim of complete testing or balanced player win rates**. In 360 seeded heuristic encounters, 114 were hero wins, 234 boss wins and 12 reached the 50-round limit. All twelve long fights were Bu’gh scenarios with continuing turns and no pending input deadlock. Starting and summoner builds perform very differently; more human playtesting is needed.

# v0.21.1 — Resonance explained

Resonance is Veyl’s stored bell power, shared by all three heroes. Higher power makes Toll of Reckoning and Hushed Chime hit harder. It persists between rounds.

- Resolving an Attack face adds 1, even if it deals zero damage. Repeated hits count once per face.
- Resolving a Skill or Defense on your own turn removes 1.
- Movement, Blanks, declines and held Reactions do not change it.
- Gathering Note and Crescendo also add power.
- Toll deals 1 + one damage per 2 power, rounded down, to each standing hero in range; it spends all power when declared.
- Chime deals 1 + one damage per 3 power, rounded down, to one hero; it spends up to 2 power when declared.
- At 4 power, Toll deals 3 per target and Chime deals 2 to one target, before Block. No legal targets means fallback with no power spent.

Open Core is separate: Crescendo raises power and exposes a weak spot until Veyl’s next turn. Hero Attack hits gain +1 damage before Block, or +2 in phase II. An effective sequence is to attack during the opening, then use Skills / Defenses to lower the next bell hit.

**In the game:** click the Resonance meter or Turn help. The scrollable guide explains the mechanic, shows current incoming damage and calculates its examples from your actual Balance Workshop values. The encounter-selection text now says what the meter means. No combat rules, probabilities or balance values changed.

Validated with the Cantor model checks, existing feedback interface checks and the real browser screen.


# Ash & Oath v0.28 — Camp, equipment and creature encounters

The game now opens on an actual main menu. Build your party, go straight to Camp, choose a creature group, start guided practice or open options/rules. Returning to Main menu keeps prepared dice and banked rewards but abandons a live encounter and its unbanked tokens. Restart party in Options also resets the party’s face choices; permanent rewards remain owned. Combat is not checkpointed.

Camp has four separate workspaces: Essence Forge, Refit dice, Equipment and Creature encounters. Crafting uses separate source, design and detail columns. Refit has larger labelled squares and an independent face picker. The starter-dice Forge selects a square first, then edits it in a separate detail panel; invalid replacements leave the build intact. Descriptions scroll within their panels. Game Options now has roomy Combat rules and Presentation columns.

Equipment provides Body, Weapon, Soul and Memory selectors for each hero. The currently available body is Broken Body; existing HP, Blank/category limits and face ownership still apply. Each hero owns one starter Dagger and their bound Beginning Soul. Existing saved equipment and earned Soul ownership are preserved. Old saves gain Beginning Soul as an available choice without replacing a previously saved equipped Soul or deliberate empty slot. New parties start with Beginning Soul equipped.
