# Ash & Oath v0.36 — six experiments in Trials

All new gameplay is available from **Main menu → Trials**. Normal Camp inventories, normal encounters, 73 normal face IDs, 48 free bonus designs and physical construction limits remain.

- **Action forecasts:** tap an ability and a target/destination, inspect HP damage after Block, Block loss/gain, healing, Poison and movement, then Confirm. Cancel returns to dice. Numeric outcomes show ranges; a known stored value stays exact. Live RNG, dice and logs are unchanged by inspection. The phone preview occupies the dice row and retains fixed Reroll and End turn controls; More → Forecast details shows longer results. Setup/copy/reaction choices retain their own input.
- **Objective routes:** stop 1 offers Break the Ritual (three 2-HP stones; surviving enemies retreat when they break). Stop 3 offers Protect the Spirit (5 HP; survive three completed enemy turns or defeat the attackers sooner). Spirit-first enemy intents match actual targeting; First Aid, Shield Wall and Taunt can protect it. Its death fails the Trial. Tap the objective banner for rules. Objectives award 3 carried Trial Essence and 2 matching crafting Essence.
- **Rolled weapon faces:** choose Spear or Hammer in the lobby. Each replaces one first normal Strike side with Jab / Crack; other attacks remain available. Jab: move at least two spaces this own turn, then deal 1 to an enemy within 2. Crack: use another Attack first, then remove up to 2 adjacent enemy Block, without HP damage. Each is once per own turn and consumes its rolled die; failed requirements spend nothing. These weapons grant no separate free Poke. Their physical side still counts as a Strike for ownership, and at least one Strike must remain equipped. Dagger variants retain their Poke effects.
- **Equipment combinations:** Poison, Movement and Protection explain actual four-slot turn sequences and costs. Apply available pieces changes only the chosen hero’s gear and lists missing pieces; it grants no missing items. Crafted additions are Fleet Body (5 HP, Movement +1), Stone Body (7 HP, Movement −1/minimum 1), Memory of Shelter (first own-turn Defense +1 Block; maximum HP −1), Hearth Soul (one Blank → heal self 1) and Wind Soul (one Blank → Move 2). Body movement changes do not change Soul conversion distance.
- **Interruptible Trial Hollow:** a visible 2-HP Rupture Core warns of Soul Rupture at the Hollow’s first action in round 2. Destroying it cancels that action. Otherwise, every standing hero/friendly summon takes 3 normal damage at any distance; Block and Reactions apply. The existing half-health phase and Roar still work. Other bosses and ordinary Hollow encounters retain their mechanics.
- **Trial Forge and wishlist:** victories bank matching Ward, Cinder or Shade Essence (regular/objective 2; elite 3); the Trial Hollow banks 3 Hollow Essence. Recipes list exact costs and encounter sources. Pin one item; victories show progress. Craft once to unlock a design for future Trial lobbies. Ingredients, crafted equipment and the wishlist persist between runs independently of carried Trial Essence and ordinary Camp. Debug’s stationary hold also unlocks Trial Forge recipes.

Old safe Trial checkpoints and boss Soul ownership remain. Leaving or reloading during a fight still fails that run; the collection remains. Reading Ram/Cutpurse intents no longer adds duplicate log entries or invalidates a target preview. Ritual/core objects have no turns, cannot move or receive Poison, and do not satisfy Dagger’s enemy-HP-damage requirement.

Current rules, PDF and in-game notes document these experiments. Balance needs human playtesting. Browser phone checks are emulation, not physical iOS/Android or speaker testing. Windows downloads were not rebuilt.
