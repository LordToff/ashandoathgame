# Ash & Oath v0.33 — room to play

Mobile combat keeps three colored ability names, Details, Reroll and End turn on screen. Secondary actions live in More. Next action and End enemy turn use the same fixed bottom control. Ability cards drop die numbers, repeated category labels and readiness prose; spent results stay dark gray. Tap an enemy ability for its effect, live target and priority. Enemy HP and Block remain visible.

Poison ticks change HP/stacks with sound and a green effect, without a popup or floating damage text. Persistent green smoke marks living poisoned heroes, bosses and summons. Exact tick outcomes remain in the battle log. Other mandatory choices and Engrave growth notices remain.

Every boss victory banks **3 matching Essence** once and opens Camp at that boss’s recipes. Each has an original earned ability costing 2 Essence per physical copy and a Memory costing 3:

| Boss | Ability | Memory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Hollow | Ember Cut: adjacent enemy takes 2; actual HP loss grants 1 Block. | Embers: first actual enemy HP damage each own turn grants 1 Block. |
| Nu’gh | Brood Needle: clear sight, reach 2, damage 1; HP loss applies Poison 1. | Brood: first Poison application each own turn adds 1 Poison; your friendly Nu’gh counts. |
| Veyl | Quiet Note: heal self 1, clear Weakness. | Silence: first resolved Skill each own turn grants 1 Block. |
| Bu’gh | Iron Shelter: gain 2 Block, or 3 if already protected by Block. | Iron: first resolved Defense each own turn grants 1 extra Block. |

One Memory slot per hero and one shared copy of each Memory type; different Memories can coexist across the party. Hollow’s automatic bound Soul reward remains. Ordinary creature token counts/prices, saved banks and the 48-design free bonus pool remain. Earned catalog IDs 69–72 extend the total to 73 faces. These boss designs are requested original prototype content; undefined PDF services/Hunts are still future work. Balance is provisional.

Enable **Debug** in Options or Camp → Collection. Hold a locked Soul/Memory card or a Forge ability recipe for 0.65 seconds to grant one item free. Dragging or releasing early cancels; ordinary taps inspect. Souls bind to the selected hero. Already-owned items do not duplicate through this shortcut. Debug saves separately, defaults off and never bypasses dice construction limits or auto-equips rewards.

Current HTML/Markdown/PDF rules and in-game patch notes cover this release. Browser and emulated touch were tested; physical phone hardware was not. Human-authored pixel art is retained. Windows downloads were not rebuilt.
