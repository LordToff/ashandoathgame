# Ash & Oath v0.40 — Four equipment slots

13 November 2026. Applies to normal play and Trials.

- **Body** sets only minimum/maximum Defense, Movement and Skill faces across all three dice. Broken: 0–3 / 0–3 / 0–3. Fleet: 0–2 / 0–4 / 0–3. Stone: 0–4 / 0–2 / 0–3. Bodies do not change HP, minimum Blanks, Attack limits or movement distance.
- **Soul** sets HP, minimum Blanks per die and its strong effect. Beginning Soul: 6 HP / 2 Blanks, Last Ember once per encounter. Soulcaster: 6 HP / 4 Blanks. Earned strong-effect Souls require 3 Blanks; Brood has 5 HP, others 6. Copies remain bound per hero.
- **Memory** starts empty. First Step Memory makes one rolled Blank Move 1 per own turn. Pure Blank conversions move from Soul to Memory. Normal First Step costs 1 Movement Creature Essence; converted Wilds Memories cost 3 matching Essence. Trial Memories can be bought for 2 Run Essence after the first victory. Memory HP penalties are removed.
- **Weapon** alone sets Attack minimum/maximum and replaces an exact printed 1–3 basic Strike sides with its attacks. All replacements are real rolled faces. Dagger / Poison Dagger: 2–5 Attacks; Heavy Dagger / Spear: 2–6; Hammer: 2–8. Common weapons replace one Strike each. Poke spends its die after earlier actual enemy HP damage; Jab / Crack keep their requirements.
- **Oathblade**, an original unique Weapon: 3–6 Attack faces, three Strikes become Oath Cut, and all equipped Attack faces gain Pierce 1. Normal recipe: 4 Iron Warden Essence. Trial Forge: 4 Ward + 2 Hollow Essence. Pierce bypasses Block without destroying the bypassed portion.
- Equipment descriptions and dice previews use the actual weapon faces. Illegal equipment/refit changes explain the unmet requirement and preserve the previous build and currency.
- Safe save migration retains currencies and owned rewards. Converted Wilds Souls become Memory copies, including multiple existing copies. Old Trial dice are repaired where necessary and backed up in the saved data; migration explains the change. First-encounter Memories are empty.

Classes remain Trials-only, with starter dice adjusted for their Body/Soul/Weapon. Skirmisher uses printed Stride to prepare Jab; Fleet no longer increases Step distance. Vanguard retains printed Move 3. Both start with Beginning Soul and empty Memory. Normal Camp and Trials keep separate collections and currencies.

Direct map targeting, compact Soulcaster controls, enemy intents, fullscreen, status feedback and licensed pixel art are retained. This is a browser update; Windows artifacts are unchanged.
