# Ash & Oath v0.35 — clear equipment and targeting

Trials now uses **Body, Memory, Soul and Weapon**, with one slot of each per hero. Four buttons show the equipped items; tap one for its description or selector. Wide screens put them in one row. The setup shows maximum HP after equipment effects. The help screen explains all four slots and previews the actual starting dice.

The former free build effects are named test Memories: Memory of Venom, Memory of Guard and Memory of Momentum. Their triggers and costs are printed on the item. Selecting a Memory changes that slot; starter dice stay fixed per hero. There is no additional passive category.

Dice edges and tempering are removed. Old selections migrate to Memories; saved dice-edge purchases refund 2 Trial Essence each, once. Existing dice, HP, route and Trial Soul ownership remain. Weapon rewards now replace the hero’s Weapon for the current Trial. Poison Dagger changes Poke to add Poison 1 after HP damage; Heavy Dagger changes Poke to 2 damage with a 1-HP cost. Both retain Dagger’s adjacent-enemy, once-per-own-turn, earlier-damage requirement and spend no die. They do not modify rolled ability faces. New Trials start with Dagger.

Selecting an ability now shows its **full board reach**, including empty tiles. Faint category color shows reach; bright outlines show targets or movement destinations; red stripes show blocked sight. Actual movement, straight Stride, Leap, range bonuses, boss footprints and sight rules are used. Inspection never changes dice or RNG. Details explains the markings on demand.

Ordinary creatures no longer draw the large boss shadow or wide boss HP label. Their HP dots and small Block badge fit the occupied cell. Actual targeting cells are outlined clearly. Two-by-two bosses retain their normal display.

**Action Reroll and Focus show the new rolled face without a text acknowledgement or Continue window.** The real dice presentation, charges, forced results, held Reactions and same-face outcomes remain. Exact old/new results are still in the battle log. Required gameplay choices remain.

Trial boss harvest feedback is also prevented from writing discoveries into ordinary Camp. Normal inventories, the 73-face catalog, 48 free bonus designs and construction limits are preserved. Current rules/PDF and in-game notes describe the new behavior. Windows downloads were not rebuilt; physical phone hardware was not tested.
