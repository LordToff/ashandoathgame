# Ash & Oath v0.28 — Camp, equipment and creature encounters

The game now opens on an actual main menu. Build your party, go straight to Camp, choose a creature group, start guided practice or open options/rules. Returning to Main menu keeps prepared dice and banked rewards but abandons a live encounter and its unbanked tokens. Restart party in Options also resets the party’s face choices; permanent rewards remain owned. Combat is not checkpointed.

Camp has four separate workspaces: Essence Forge, Refit dice, Equipment and Creature encounters. Crafting uses separate source, design and detail columns. Refit has larger labelled squares and an independent face picker. The starter-dice Forge selects a square first, then edits it in a separate detail panel; invalid replacements leave the build intact. Descriptions scroll within their panels. Game Options now has roomy Combat rules and Presentation columns.

Equipment provides Body, Weapon, Soul and Memory selectors for each hero. The currently available body is Broken Body; existing HP, Blank/category limits and face ownership still apply. Each hero owns one starter Dagger and their bound Beginning Soul. Existing saved equipment and earned Soul ownership are preserved. Old saves gain Beginning Soul as an available choice without replacing a previously saved equipped Soul or deliberate empty slot. New parties start with Beginning Soul equipped.

- **Beginning Soul:** spend one rolled Action Blank as Move 1, once per own turn. No free movement, no installed-face change. Invalid movement, rerolls and sandbox toggles never refill/spend the allowance. Normal and full recovery turns refresh it. Rooted prevents voluntary movement. Current construction still requires at least two Blanks per die. Hollow and Ash Road Souls retain their stronger Move 2 conversion, scoped to the equipped hero. The optional sandbox override remains separate.
- **Dagger — Poke:** base 1 normal Attack damage to one adjacent enemy, only after the acting hero has caused actual enemy HP loss during this own turn. Once per own turn; no Action Die or reroll spent. A blocked hit, friendly fire, another hero’s hit, a summon’s hit or a Shared Burden interrupt does not unlock it. Finish pending/forced actions first. No friendly Poke targets. Normal Attack modifiers, Weakness, Block, armor, Mark/Bargain/Weak Point and Veyl’s exposed-core bonus apply; it charges Veyl’s Resonance as an Attack. It can be used after all dice are spent. Starter availability and the once-per-turn/no-die cost are explicit prototype choices for the newly requested weapon.
- **Creature encounters:** select Bone Patrol (two 3-HP Bone Creatures), Mixed Group (one 2-HP Support + two 3-HP Movement Creatures), or Swift Pack (two 3-HP Movement Creatures) directly from Camp/Main menu. Incomplete parties are routed through the existing two-bonus-face selection, then return to the chosen fight. A direct win banks each individual’s normal Essence once; defeat/abandoning loses only encounter tokens. Direct fights grant no event Gold, supplies, Fragments or boss Souls and do not resolve/skip a required pre-boss event. Existing random creature events and all four bosses remain.
- **Smoother dice presentation:** available Action Dice roll together in one animated panel. Numeric Dice retain a separate result presentation. Rotations slow to a stop, the landing follows a smooth curve, and queued animations preserve elapsed time. Every final side/result comes from the model; animation never rerolls or changes outcomes. Input waits for the presentation to finish, and queued notices follow it.
- **Item art:** distinct transparent sprites for Beginning Soul, Hollow Soul, Ash Road Soul and Memory of Loss. Owned items display in full colour; undiscovered items stay dimmed and unnamed. Memory effects still reveal only after crafting. Creature cards display a single sprite frame rather than an entire animation sheet.

AI/statistics and the new direct group composition remain provisional. Undefined services, campaign Hunt scheduling and unique-boss unlock opportunities are not invented. No Windows export was rebuilt.
